### Mark-scheme conventions observed (all five sessions)
- Structure: "Question Number | Answer | Mark" tables; each mark point is a bullet line ending "(1)"; part totals printed as "(4)", "(3)" etc.; question totals "Total for question 17   10". Alternative methods marked "Either … Or …"; alternatives for a single point joined by "Or" on the next line.
- Mark ranges: "Max 6" style caps where more points than marks are listed (2019-Jun Q17(b) lists 7 points, Max 6).
- Dependent/conditional marks printed in round brackets under the points, e.g. "(MP4 conditional on scoring MP1 & MP2 & MP3)".
- Starred (*) questions: full QoWC apparatus — "Marks are awarded for indicative content and for how the answer is structured and shows lines of reasoning", IC-point→mark conversion table (6 IC → 4 marks, 5–4 → 3, 3–2 → 2, 1 → 1, 0 → 0) plus structure marks (2 for coherent/logical with min 5 linked IC points, 1 for partial, 0 for none). 2019-Oct Q14 uses a variant table with a "Max linkage mark" column (6 IC → 4 + 2 linkage = 6).
- "Show that" questions: numeric ranges accepted ("k in range 22 – 26 N m-1", "E28/ E2 = 1.30 to 1.40", "Percentage reduction = 12.0 % to 15.0 %", "E = (1.2 – 1.3) × 1011 Pa", "Esteel = 1.5 × 1011 (Pa) to 1.8 × 1011 (Pa)", "v = 6.5 – 7.0 m s−1", "Breaking stress = 5.00 to 5.10 (×108 Pa)").
- Every calculation part ends with "Example of calculation" showing full substitution with units (e.g. "F = 6 × π × 3.0 × 10−3 m × 8.9 × 10−4 Pa s × 0.50 m s−1").
- General guidance boilerplate (identical in all five MS): unit error penalty not a separate mark but loses the final calculation mark unless "[no ue]"; case of unit symbols not penalised; no unit penalty in "show that" questions; same missing unit penalised once per question; sig-fig penalties only in "show that" answers; g = 10 instead of 9.81 penalised once per paper (9.8 accepted); bald correct answers score full marks except in "show that"; reverse working in a 2-mark "show that" scores 2, in 3-mark only 2 of 3; QoWC = final mark within a max; graph marks: axes labelled with quantity+unit, points within 1 mm with the stated 1 mm/2 mm checking rule, best-fit thin continuous line.
- MCQ MS style: "X is the correct answer" (+ "The only correct answer is X because …" in 2019-Jun/2020 files), followed by one rationale line per distractor ("A is not the correct answer as …").
